This policy applies to the Android app “MSR PDF” (the “app”). The developer is the controller for the data processing described below.

2. Your PDF files, scans and inputs

All functions for opening, editing, merging, splitting, converting, compressing, protecting and saving PDFs run on your device. Your files, their contents and everything you type into them are not uploaded and not sent to us.

Text recognition (OCR) also runs on your device. The document scanner uses the camera only while the scanner screen is open. Captured images are processed on your device only and are not transmitted.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally in its protected app storage:

  • recently opened files (name and reference),
  • unsaved changes as a draft and the last reading position per document,
  • your settings (for example theme and language),
  • if you use “Auto-fill”: the details you enter for it (for example name, address, contact details, date of birth, IBAN, answers to form questions) and your signature as an image,
  • temporary working copies in the cache.

These details are stored unencrypted in the app's protected storage and never leave your device through the app. You can delete saved auto-fill details in the app settings (“Delete saved details”), or remove all app data by clearing the app's storage in the Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, for example before saving a document, unless you have the “Remove ads” subscription. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process data such as your IP address, device information and your advertising ID. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice later in the app settings under “Privacy settings for ads” (shown where required).

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Subscription (Google Play)

The optional “Remove ads” subscription is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment data. Through Google Play we only learn whether a subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Google Play under “Payments & subscriptions”.

6. Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, only the text you typed is sent to us by e-mail through the form service Web3Forms (web3forms.com). For technical reasons the service also receives connection data such as your IP address. The app does not add your name, e-mail address or device details. Please do not enter personal data you do not want to share. We use feedback only to improve the app and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.

7. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: for the document scanner, only while it is open.
  • Internet and network state: to show ads, check the subscription and send feedback. Editing PDFs does not need a connection.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for ads, subject to your consent where required.

8. No accounts, no own tracking, no sale of data

There is no registration or login. We do not use our own analytics or tracking tools and do not sell your personal data. Third-party services named in this policy (Google AdMob, Google Play, Web3Forms) process data under their own privacy policies.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Storage and deletion

Data on your device stays there until you delete it (see section 3). Feedback e-mails are deleted when they are no longer needed. Data processed by Google and Web3Forms is stored according to their policies.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details in section 1.

12. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Advertising and access to device identifiers: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, section 25 TDDDG), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Subscription: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Feedback: our legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

13. International data transfers

Google and Web3Forms may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where required, such transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision, as described in their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The current version is always available at this address and in the app under Settings → Privacy. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
